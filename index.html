04 FAQ

Asked often.
Answered straight.

What does Ravolution actually do?

It builds digital products, sold through the Vaults, and it builds for small businesses: NeverMissed, an AI phone line for takeaways, and Ravolution Web, websites designed and built for local businesses. Everything is designed, built and shipped by one person: Raki.

What is in the Vaults?

Written guides across five shelves: Fitness & Body, Make Money, AI & Automation, Mindset & Discipline and Health & Longevity. The first, Sculpted: The 12-Week Aesthetic Blueprint, is on sale now, with more landing one by one. Each guide is a PDF you download instantly and keep.

Are the Vaults open?

Yes. Any guide with a price and a Buy now button can be bought today. Payment goes through Stripe and the download lands in your inbox straight after. Guides marked Coming soon have a Notify me button — one email the day each one lands. Enter the Vaults.

How do I get a product after buying?

Instantly. As soon as payment clears you get an email with a permanent download link. Lost the email? Head to Your Library and it will be resent within one working day.

What is your refund policy?

Every Vault guide carries a 30-day money-back guarantee. Request it on the Refunds & Delivery page or email rehan@ravolution.agency. Every request is checked personally, and approved refunds go back to your original payment method, normally within 5 to 10 working days.

What is NeverMissed and who is it for?

NeverMissed is an AI phone system for takeaways and small food businesses. It answers every call, takes the full order and prints it in the kitchen like any other ticket, every hour you're open, so orders stop dying on a ringing phone. £499 a month by Direct Debit or card, first week free, then a 3-month minimum with one month's notice to cancel. See how it works.

Can you build something custom for my business?

Sometimes. If it is an automation or AI system close to what already runs here, send a short note through the form below describing the problem rather than the tool. You will get an honest answer on whether it is a fit.

How fast do you reply?

Replies within one working day (Mon–Fri, UK time). Refund requests processed within two working days.
