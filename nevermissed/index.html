02 How it works

Live in your shop
in under an hour

  1. 01

    Divert your phone

    Busy or unanswered calls forward to your NeverMissed number. Takes 2 minutes, works with any landline or mobile. Your customers still ring the same number they always have.

  2. 02

    AI takes the order

    A natural, friendly voice greets customers by your shop's name, knows your full menu and prices, takes the order, and confirms it back — collection or delivery.

    Ticket in your kitchen

    We install a small box and a ticket printer in your kitchen. The second the call ends, the full order prints like any other ticket — name, items, total and time. Text alerts to your phone are available too.

05 Questions owners ask

Fair questions,
straight answers

Will customers know it's AI?

It sounds natural, and it never pretends to be a person: if a caller asks, it says it's the shop's automated order line. I can also add a short line to the greeting so every caller knows up front. What customers care about is getting through and getting their food right.

What if it gets an order wrong?

It repeats every order back to the customer before ending the call, and you see the full order on the printed ticket. Anything unusual, it takes the customer's number so you can ring back — it never guesses.

Do I need new equipment?

No new phone system. It works with your existing line — we just set up call diverting — and we bring a small ticket printer for the kitchen and connect it as part of setup. If you also want orders texted to your phone, that can be added too.

What about my menu changing?

Text us the change and it's live the same day. Price updates, specials, items off tonight — all handled.

Can it just cover busy times or after hours?

Yes. Many shops only divert when the line's engaged or unanswered — so it's a safety net, not a replacement. You choose.

How do I get started?

Leave your shop name and number in the form below. I ring you back within one working day, we book a visit, and you're live the same afternoon. Before I leave we set up your billing on the spot (card or Direct Debit), and your free week starts that day. After that it's a simple monthly plan with a 3-month minimum.
