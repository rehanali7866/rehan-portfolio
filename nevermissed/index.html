05 QUESTIONS OWNERS ASK

FAIR QUESTIONS,
straight ANSWERS

Will customers know it's AI?

It sounds natural and most callers don't notice — but it never pretends. If a caller asks, it says it's the shop's automated order line. What customers care about is getting through and getting their food right.

What if it gets an order wrong?

It repeats every order back to the customer before ending the call, and you see the full order in the text. Anything unusual, it takes the customer's number so you can ring back — it never guesses.

Do I need new equipment?

No. It works with your existing phone line — we just set up call diverting. If you later want orders on a kitchen screen or receipt printer, we can add that.

What about my menu changing?

Text us the change and it's live the same day. Price updates, specials, items off tonight — all handled.

Can it just cover busy times or after hours?

Yes. Many shops only divert when the line's engaged or unanswered — so it's a safety net, not a replacement. You choose.
