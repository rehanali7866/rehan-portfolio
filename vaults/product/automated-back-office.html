The Automated Back Office: Workflows for Enquiries, Bookings and Follow-Up

Take the repetitive admin out of your week, one workflow at a time.

A practical programme for automating the admin behind a one-person operation: deciding what is actually worth automating, mapping a process properly before you build it, and wiring enquiries, quotes, bookings, follow-ups and records between the tools you already pay for.
