About this guide

A complete build guide for an AI phone receptionist, written from a system in daily use with small businesses. Covers designing the call flow, writing prompts that hold up against real callers, connecting the voice layer to a phone number, capturing and routing the enquiry, sending the follow-up text, handing over to a human, and what it costs to run per call. The last third is about failure: what breaks, how to hear about it before your customer does, and how to test against messy calls rather than tidy ones.
