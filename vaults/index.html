Before you buy.

How do I get a guide after I buy it?

As soon as payment clears you get an email with a permanent PDF download link. Lost it? Head to Your Library and it will be resent within one working day.

What format are the guides in?

Every guide is a PDF, so it opens on any phone, tablet or laptop. Kindle readers can send it to their Kindle too.

What is the refund policy?

Every guide carries a 30-day money-back guarantee. Request it on the Refunds page or by email. Every request is checked personally, and once it is approved the money goes back to your original payment method, normally within 5 to 10 working days.

Can I buy more than one at a time?

Yes, one after the other. Each guide checks out on its own through Stripe, so buy one, then come back for the next. Every guide lands in your inbox separately.

Can I buy today?

Yes. Any guide with a price and a Buy now button is available right now. Payment goes through Stripe and the download lands in your inbox straight after. Guides marked Coming soon have a Notify me button — one email the day each one lands.
