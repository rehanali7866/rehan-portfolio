Who we are

RAVOLUTION LTD ("we", "us", "our") is a company registered in Scotland, United Kingdom.

We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us at any time at rehan@ravolution.agency.

What we collect

Website and enquiries

  • Contact messages — your name (if you give it), email address and the content of your message, so we can reply.
  • Mailing list and waitlists — if you join The Dispatch or a product waitlist, your email address and the list you joined.
  • Support and refund requests — your email address, your order reference and the details of the request, so we can act on it.
  • Website visits — aggregate, cookie-free statistics such as page views, referring site, country and device type. None of it identifies you. See Cookies & local storage below.

Customers of our products

  • Order and billing details — name, business name, email, billing address and the products purchased. We do not see or store full payment card numbers; payments are handled by our payment provider.
  • Business contact details — for subscribing businesses, the details needed to configure and support the service.

NeverMissed call data

NeverMissed is an AI phone receptionist used by businesses such as takeaways and restaurants. When a business subscribes, calls to that business's own published phone number are answered by our automated assistant.

  • We process the content of those calls — the order or request, and any delivery address or callback number the caller provides — solely to produce the order ticket the business needs to fulfil it.
  • Call audio and transcripts are processed to deliver the service and to diagnose faults.
  • Where a subscribing business uses the service, that business is the controller of its own customers' data and we act as its processor.
  • We do not sell call data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Why we use it

To provide the services you or your business have asked for, to fulfil orders, to reply to enquiries, to take payment, to keep proper business records, and to meet our legal obligations. Where we rely on consent — such as a mailing list — you may withdraw it at any time.

Where we rely on our legitimate interests, such as measuring how the website is used in aggregate or protecting our forms from abuse, we have balanced those interests against your rights and keep the data to the minimum needed.

Who we share it with

We share personal data only with the suppliers needed to run the service, under contract and only for that purpose. We do not sell personal data to anyone. Our processors are:

  • Hosting and analytics — Vercel. Hosts this website and provides Vercel Web Analytics, which is cookie-free and records aggregate visits only.
  • Form relay — FormSubmit. Contact, newsletter and waitlist submissions pass through it to our inbox. It receives what you type into the form, your email address and the address of the page you sent it from, and forwards them to us.
  • Email — an EU-hosted business mailbox. Where enquiries, orders and support requests are received, answered and stored.
  • NeverMissed — telephony, voice-AI and workflow providers. A telephony provider carries the calls and text messages, a voice-AI provider understands the caller and takes the order, and a workflow provider turns the call into the order ticket. Call content passes through these providers only to deliver the service.
  • Payment provider. When the Vaults store opens, payment is taken by a PCI-DSS compliant payment provider. We never see or store full card numbers; we receive confirmation of the order, the amount paid and the details needed to deliver your purchase.

If we change a supplier, we update this page.

Where it is stored

Our email and business data are held in the European Union. Some suppliers may process data outside the UK or EEA; where they do, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or equivalent contractual protections.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for up to 24 months. Order and billing records are kept for six years, as UK tax law requires. Call transcripts and order tickets are retained only as long as needed to deliver and support the service, and are deleted on request by the subscribing business unless we must keep them by law. Mailing list addresses are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of these, email rehan@ravolution.agency. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you may complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies & local storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Here is exactly what it stores on your device and why.

  • Essential local storage. The Vaults store keeps your basket in your browser's local storage so it survives a page refresh, and the site remembers the choice you make in the cookies & privacy banner so we do not keep asking. Neither leaves your device and neither identifies you.
  • Cookie-free analytics. We measure visits with Vercel Web Analytics. It sets no cookies, stores no identifier on your device and does not follow you across other sites. Because it cannot identify you, it counts as essential and runs whichever banner option you choose.
  • Optional analytics. We do not currently run any. If we ever add a tool that sets cookies or an identifier, it will load only after you choose Accept all in the banner, and you can withdraw that choice at any time.

On your first visit the banner asks you to choose between Accept all and Essential only. You can change your mind whenever you like: use here, or the same link in the footer of every page. Clearing your browser's site data removes the basket and your choice, and the banner will appear again.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
